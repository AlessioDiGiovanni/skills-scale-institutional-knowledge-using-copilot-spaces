# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Use this guide as a single entry point to OctoAcme's project management processes and role guidance.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme delivers customer value through small, testable increments. Initiation establishes the business need, success metrics, stakeholders, initial risks, and a project one-pager before a go/no-go decision. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, owners, dependencies, and release milestones. Teams track execution on a project board from Backlog through Review and QA to Done, then release, verify outcomes, and capture improvements in retrospectives.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication. Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the roadmap and backlog, and measure success. Developers design, build, test, document, and review changes; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria. Stakeholders provide input and approvals, while Product Leads and sponsors support alignment and escalated decisions.

Communication combines team standups at an agreed cadence, weekly delivery and PM/Product Manager syncs, sprint or milestone demos, and regular stakeholder updates. Shared project documentation provides a single source of truth for progress, next steps, risks, and decisions needed. Teams maintain a risk register with owners and mitigation plans, review it weekly, and escalate blockers from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor.

Quality assurance includes small, reviewed pull requests, automated tests and linting, CI security scans, and manual acceptance testing when needed. Releases require satisfied acceptance criteria, passing checks, release notes, and a rollback plan, followed by staging smoke tests, production verification, and stakeholder announcements. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents turn lessons into prioritized backlog actions with owners and due dates; teams track follow-through and measure their impact.
